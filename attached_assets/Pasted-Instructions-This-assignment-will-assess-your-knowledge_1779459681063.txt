Assignment: System Testing, Maintenance Planning, and Project Reporting 

This assignment focuses on the final stages of the software development process—testing, maintenance planning, and reporting. You will test the integrated system to ensure its reliability, create a plan for post-deployment maintenance, and develop a preliminary version of your final project report summarizing your work to date. 

Part A: System Testing and Documentation 

Conduct comprehensive testing of your integrated software system to verify its functionality, performance, and stability. In this regard, perform the following three tasks: 

System Testing: 

Execute multiple levels of testing such as integration, system, and acceptance testing as appropriate for your project. 

Include screenshots or log files that demonstrate testing in action and outputs for passed/failed test cases. 

Test Documentation: 

Prepare a test summary report outlining the testing objectives, scope, test cases, procedures, and results. 

Identify any bugs, issues, or system limitations found during testing, and explain how these were resolved or documented for future improvement. 

Evaluation: 

Reflect briefly on how comprehensive testing contributes to ensuring the reliability and quality of your project. 

Part B: System Maintenance Plan 

Prepare a maintenance plan to ensure the system’s long-term performance and adaptability post-deployment. In this regard, complete the following three tasks: 

Maintenance Strategy: 

Discuss different types of maintenance, corrective, adaptive, perfective, and preventive, and their relevance to your project. 

Outline a proposed schedule or approach for monitoring, updates, and performance assessments. 

Maintenance Documentation: 

Describe how you will manage version updates, bug fixes, and enhancement requests. 

Explain how version control and configuration management tools will support your maintenance strategy. 

Risk Identification and Mitigation: 

Identify likely post-deployment risks (e.g., security vulnerabilities, data inconsistencies, or usability issues). 

Suggest appropriate mitigation or contingency measures to manage these risks effectively.
